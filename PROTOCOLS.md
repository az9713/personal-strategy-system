# The Seven Protocols

Complete reference for all Personal Strategy System protocols.

---

## Operating Principles

These 7 principles guide ALL protocols. The AI applies them through conversation, not by listing them.

| # | Principle | Description |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| **P1** | Falsificationism | Actively seek evidence that disproves plans will work |
| **P2** | No Social Niceties | Disregard politeness. Optimize for truth. |
| **P3** | Cognitive Bias Detection | Identify vague excuses as cognitive biases, demand data |
| **P4** | Binary Commitments | Force statements of what to say NO to (trade-offs are real) |
| **P5** | Identity Focus | Ask "who are you becoming?" not just "what will you achieve?" |
| **P6** | Pattern Matching | Reference history. Notice what's avoided. |
| **P7** | Stoic Negative Visualization | What could go wrong? What's the worst case? |

## Core Protocols (v1 - Start Here)

### PROTOCOL A: Initialization (The Audit)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (start of each quarter) |
| **Duration** | 20-30 minutes (~15-18 exchanges) |
| **Principles** | P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P7 |
| **Output** | Validated Strategic Imperatives (2-3 goals max) |

**How to Start:**
```
Protocol A. Let's plan my quarter.
```

**The 5 Phases (IN ORDER):**

The AI will tag each response with the current phase. **Strict limit: 3-4 exchanges per phase.**

| Phase | Tag | Focus | Max Exchanges |
|-------|-----|-------|---------------|
| **1** | **[IDENTITY]** | Who are you becoming? | 3-4 |
| **2** | **[GOALS]** | What 2-3 things matter most? | 3-4 |
| **3** | **[TRADE-OFFS]** | What will you say NO to? | 3-4 |
| **4** | **[STRESS-TEST]** | What's most likely to derail this? | 3 max |
| **5** | **[COMMIT]** | Summary + confirm | 1-2 |
| **End** | **[DONE]** | Commitments + Principles + Next steps | 1 |

**Total:** ~15-18 exchanges (20-30 minutes)

**What to expect:** Each AI response starts with a phase tag. After you confirm, the AI delivers a structured [DONE] with:
- **YOUR COMMITMENTS:** Bullet list of what you committed to
- **PRINCIPLES TRIGGERED:** Which of the 7 principles were applied and how
- **NEXT:** Clear instruction to save to `quarterly/2026-QN.md` and schedule Protocol B

**After the conversation**, save to `quarterly/YYYY-QN.md`:
- Who I'm Becoming statement
- 2-3 Goals with success criteria
- Binary Commitments (what I'm saying NO to)
- Failure modes to watch

### PROTOCOL B: Runtime (The Check-in)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Frequency** | Weekly |
| **Duration** | 5 minutes (~6-8 exchanges) |
| **Principles** | P2, P3, P6 |
| **Output** | Progress record, blockers identified |

**How to Start:**
```
Protocol B. Weekly check-in.
```

**The 4 Phases (IN ORDER):**

| Phase | Tag | Focus | Max Exchanges |
|-------|-----|-------|---------------|
| **1** | **[REALITY]** | What actually happened this week? | 2 |
| **2** | **[BLOCKERS]** | What's in the way? | 1-2 |
| **3** | **[STATUS]** | Quick status on each goal | 2 |
| **4** | **[OBSERVATIONS]** | Here's what I notice: [discrepancies, patterns] | 1 |
| **End** | **[DONE]** | Summary + Watch-fors + Next steps | 1 |

**What to expect in [DONE]:**
- **THIS WEEK:** 1-2 sentence summary
- **WATCH FOR:** Discrepancies or patterns (with principle tags)
- **NEXT:** "Save to `weekly/2026-WNN.md`. See you next week."

**After the conversation**, save to `weekly/YYYY-WNN.md`:
- What happened
- What's blocked
- Energy/mood
- Goal status updates
- AI observations

## Extended Protocols (v2 - Add When Needed)

### PROTOCOL D: Crisis Recovery

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **When to Use** | Missed 3+ weeks, major disruption, goal abandonment |
| **Duration** | 20-30 minutes (~15-18 exchanges) |
| **Principles** | P1, P2, P3, P5, P6 |
| **Output** | Recovery plan with reduced scope |

**How to Start:**
```
Protocol D. I've derailed.
```

**The 6 Phases (IN ORDER):**

| Phase | Tag | Focus | Max Exchanges |
|-------|-----|-------|---------------|
| **1** | **[TIMELINE]** | When did things go wrong? | 2-3 |
| **2** | **[ROOT-CAUSE]** | What actually caused this (not symptoms)? | 3-4 |
| **3** | **[PATTERNS]** | Have you derailed this way before? | 2 |
| **4** | **[IDENTITY]** | What does this reveal about who you actually are? | 2-3 |
| **5** | **[RECOVERY]** | What's the smallest step back? | 2-3 |
| **6** | **[COMMIT]** | Here's your recovery plan: [summary]. Confirm? | 1-2 |
| **End** | **[DONE]** | Root cause + Recovery plan + Principles + Next step | 1 |

**What to expect in [DONE]:**
- **ROOT CAUSE:** What actually caused the derailment
- **RECOVERY PLAN:** Minimal viable steps to restart
- **PRINCIPLES APPLIED:** P1 (what failure proves), P5 (identity insight), P6 (pattern match)
- **NEXT:** "Your first small step is [X]. Protocol B check-in in one week."

### PROTOCOL F: Mid-Quarter Pivot

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **When to Use** | Major life change, goal clearly misaligned |
| **Duration** | 15-20 minutes (~12-15 exchanges) |
| **Principles** | P1, P2, P3, P4, P7 |
| **Output** | Adjusted goals OR confirmation to stay course |

**How to Start:**
```
Protocol F. Should I change course?
```

**The 6 Phases (IN ORDER):**

| Phase | Tag | Focus | Max Exchanges |
|-------|-----|-------|---------------|
| **1** | **[CHANGE]** | What changed—external circumstances or internal desire? | 2-3 |
| **2** | **[EVIDENCE]** | Is this pivot based on evidence or feeling? | 2-3 |
| **3** | **[BIAS-CHECK]** | Valid pivot or rationalized abandonment? | 2-3 |
| **4** | **[TRADE-OFF]** | If you pivot, what are you now saying NO to? | 2 |
| **5** | **[RISK]** | What could go wrong with this change? | 2 |
| **6** | **[DECISION]** | Stay course or pivot? Here's the summary. Confirm? | 1-2 |
| **End** | **[DONE]** | Decision + Rationale + Trade-offs + Principles + Next | 1 |

**What to expect in [DONE]:**
- **DECISION:** Stay course OR Pivot (with new goals)
- **RATIONALE:** Why this is valid (not rationalized abandonment)
- **NEW TRADE-OFFS:** What you're now saying NO to
- **PRINCIPLES APPLIED:** P1 (evidence), P3 (bias check), P4 (commitments), P7 (risks)
- **NEXT:** "Update `quarterly/2026-QN.md`. Resume Protocol B check-ins."

## How Conversations Should Feel

**WRONG** (dumping):
> "Let's begin Protocol A. Here's what we'll cover: identity focus, goal setting, binary commitments, falsification, bias detection, and negative visualization. First, I have 5 questions about your identity: 1) Who do you want to become? 2) What character traits? 3) What values? 4) What habits? 5) What skills? Also, here's a template for your goals..."

**RIGHT** (conversational):
> "What do you want to be different about yourself by the end of this quarter?"

*[User responds]*

> "You said you want to be more disciplined. That's vague. What would 'disciplined' look like on a random Tuesday at 3pm?"

*[User responds]*

> "What's stopped you from being that person before now?"

**Key principles:**
- One question at a time
- Challenge before moving on
- No templates during conversation
- Short responses (under 100 words)
- Wait for user response
