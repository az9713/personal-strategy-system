# Quick Start Guide

Get started with the Personal Strategy System in 5 minutes, plus 10 real-world use cases to try.

---

## 5-Minute Quick Start

### Step 1: Copy the System Prompt (30 seconds)

1. Open `SYSTEM.md` in this folder
2. Select all (Ctrl+A / Cmd+A)
3. Copy (Ctrl+C / Cmd+C)

### Step 2: Paste into AI (30 seconds)

1. Go to [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) or [Claude](https://claude.ai)
2. Start a new chat
3. Paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V)
4. Send the message

### Step 3: Start Your First Protocol (4 minutes)

Type this and send:

```
Protocol A. Let's plan my quarter.
```

Answer the AI's questions honestly. It will challenge you—that's the point!

### Step 4: Save Your Results

When you see `[DONE]`, copy the output and save it to `quarterly/2026-Q1.md`.

**That's it!** You've completed your first session.

## 10 Use Cases to Try

These are real-world scenarios to help you learn the system. Try them in order for the best learning experience.

---

### Use Case 1: "I Want to Exercise More" (Protocol A)

**The Problem:** This is the most common vague goal that fails every year.

**How to Try It:**

1. Paste SYSTEM.md into AI
2. Say: `Protocol A. Let's plan my quarter.`
3. When asked about identity, say: "I want to exercise more"

**What You'll Learn:**
- How the AI pushes back on vague goals
- How to turn "exercise more" into specific, measurable commitments
- How to identify what you'll say NO to (trade-offs)

**Expected Outcome:**
Instead of "exercise more," you'll end up with something like:
- Identity: "I am the kind of person who moves their body every morning before checking email"
- Goal: "Run 3x per week for 20 minutes minimum"
- Trade-off: "I will NOT snooze my alarm"
- Failure mode: "Telling myself 'I'll do it later' when I wake up tired"

## Tips for Success

### Do This

- **Be honest**: The AI can only help if you tell the truth
- **Be specific**: "3 times per week" not "more often"
- **Accept discomfort**: The AI's challenges are helping you
- **Save everything**: Your patterns emerge from history

### Don't Do This

- **Don't argue with the format**: Trust the phases
- **Don't skip the trade-offs**: Every goal has a cost
- **Don't ignore patterns**: If the AI notices something, pay attention
- **Don't expect perfection**: Progress matters more than perfect execution
