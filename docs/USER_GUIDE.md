## 1. What is the Personal Strategy System?

### The Problem with Traditional Goal-Setting

Most people set goals like this:
- "I want to exercise more"
- "I want to learn a new skill"
- "I want to be more productive"

These goals fail because:
- They're vague (what does "more" mean?)
- They don't address trade-offs (what will you give up?)
- They don't account for your history (why didn't this work before?)
- You're too easy on yourself (no one challenges your excuses)

### The Solution: An AI That Challenges You

The Personal Strategy System uses AI (like ChatGPT or Claude) as an **adversarial coach**. This means:

- The AI is **not** your cheerleader
- The AI is **not** your therapist
- The AI **will** challenge your vague goals
- The AI **will** call out your excuses
- The AI **will** force you to be specific

### What You'll Get

After using this system, you'll have:
- Clear, measurable goals (not vague wishes)
- Specific things you're saying NO to (trade-offs)
- Known failure modes (what might derail you)
- A weekly check-in habit
- Documented patterns about yourself

## 3. Your First Session: Protocol A

Protocol A is your **quarterly planning session**. Do this at the start of each quarter (January, April, July, October) or whenever you want to set new goals.

### When to Use Protocol A

- Start of a new quarter
- Starting fresh after a long break
- Major life change requiring new goals

### Duration

20-30 minutes

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Open SYSTEM.md

1. Navigate to your `personal-strategy` folder
2. Open `SYSTEM.md` with your text editor
3. Select all the text (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on Mac)
4. Copy it (Ctrl+C on Windows, Cmd+C on Mac)

#### Step 2: Start a New AI Chat

1. Go to ChatGPT or Claude
2. Start a new conversation
3. Paste the SYSTEM.md content (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)
4. Press Enter/Send

The AI will receive the instructions but won't do anything yet.

#### Step 3: Trigger Protocol A

Type this message and send it:

```
Protocol A. Let's plan my quarter.
```

#### Step 4: Have the Conversation

The AI will ask you questions, one at a time. Answer honestly.

**What to expect:**

The AI will show phase tags like `[IDENTITY]`, `[GOALS]`, etc.

**Example exchange:**

```
AI: [IDENTITY] What do you want to be different about yourself by end of quarter?

You: I want to be healthier.

AI: [IDENTITY] "Healthier" is vague. What observable behavior would prove—without
interpretation—that you are healthier by quarter's end?

You: I would exercise 3 times a week.

AI: [IDENTITY] That's still a goal, not an identity. Who are you becoming?
Finish: "I am the kind of person who..."

You: I am the kind of person who prioritizes physical movement even when busy.

AI: [GOALS] Identity noted. What 2-3 concrete things do you want to accomplish?
```

#### Step 5: Complete All Phases

The conversation will go through these phases:
1. `[IDENTITY]` - Who are you becoming?
2. `[GOALS]` - What 2-3 things matter?
3. `[TRADE-OFFS]` - What are you saying NO to?
4. `[STRESS-TEST]` - What could derail this?
5. `[COMMIT]` - Confirm your plan
6. `[DONE]` - Final summary

#### Step 6: Save Your Results

When you see `[DONE]`, copy the entire output.

1. Open your `quarterly` folder
2. Copy `TEMPLATE.md` and rename it (e.g., `2026-Q1.md`)
3. Paste the AI's summary into the file
4. Fill in any additional sections

## 4. Weekly Check-ins: Protocol B

Protocol B is a **quick weekly check-in**. Do this every week to track progress.

### When to Use Protocol B

- Every week (pick a consistent day, like Sunday or Friday)
- After completing tasks
- When you want to reflect on your week

### Duration

5 minutes

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Prepare Your Context

For the best results, include your recent check-ins:

1. Open SYSTEM.md
2. At the bottom, paste your last 2-3 weekly check-ins
3. Copy the entire content

#### Step 2: Start the AI Chat

1. Go to ChatGPT or Claude
2. Start a new conversation (or continue an existing one)
3. Paste the SYSTEM.md content (with your check-ins)

#### Step 3: Trigger Protocol B

Type:

```
Protocol B. Weekly check-in.
```

#### Step 4: Answer the Questions

**Example exchange:**

```
AI: [REALITY] What actually happened this week?

You: I worked on my project 3 days, but skipped 2 days. I also watched more
YouTube than planned.

AI: [BLOCKERS] What got in the way?

You: I had unexpected meetings and felt tired after work.

AI: [STATUS] Quick update on each goal?

You: Goal 1 (deploy apps): Deployed 1 app, behind target.
Goal 2 (YouTube cap): Watched 35 hours instead of 28.

AI: [OBSERVATIONS] I notice you mentioned "tired after work" - this is the
second week in a row. Also, you're 1 app behind and 7 hours over on YouTube.
The pattern suggests evening energy is your bottleneck.

AI: [DONE]

**THIS WEEK:** Made progress on 1 app but fell behind on both targets.
Energy after work is a recurring issue.

**WATCH FOR:**
- P6 Pattern: Evening fatigue blocking goals (2 weeks running)
- P3 Bias: "Unexpected meetings" may be excuse—were they truly unexpected?

**NEXT:** Save to `weekly/2026-W02.md`. See you next week.
```

#### Step 5: Save Your Check-in

1. Open your `weekly` folder
2. Copy `TEMPLATE.md` and rename it (e.g., `2026-W02.md`)
3. Fill in the sections based on your conversation

## 5. Monthly Pattern Analysis: Protocol C

Protocol C looks at your check-ins to find **patterns** you might not notice yourself.

### When to Use Protocol C

- After 4-6 weekly check-ins
- When you feel stuck but don't know why
- At the end of each month

### Duration

15-20 minutes

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Gather Your Check-ins

1. Open all your weekly check-in files from the past month
2. Copy the content from each one

#### Step 2: Prepare SYSTEM.md

1. Open SYSTEM.md
2. At the bottom, paste all your check-ins
3. Copy the entire content

#### Step 3: Trigger Protocol C

Type:

```
Protocol C. What patterns do you see?
```

#### Step 4: Explore the Patterns

The AI will ask about:
- **Energy patterns**: When do you have energy? When do you crash?
- **Avoidance patterns**: What goals do you keep skipping?
- **Blocker patterns**: What obstacles keep appearing?
- **Success patterns**: What helps you follow through?
- **Bias patterns**: What excuses do you repeat?

#### Step 5: Update patterns.md

After the conversation, open `patterns.md` and add your findings:

```markdown
## Energy Patterns
- Crashes every evening after 7pm
- Peak productivity between 9-11am
- Mondays consistently low energy

## Avoidance Patterns
- Goal 2 mentioned in W01, not mentioned since
- Always deflects exercise discussion with "busy"

## Recurring Blockers
- "Unexpected meetings" cited 4 times
- "Tired after work" cited 3 times
```

## 6. Other Protocols (D, E, F, G)

### Protocol D: Crisis Recovery

**When to use:** You've completely derailed (missed 3+ weeks, abandoned goals)

**How to start:**
```
Protocol D. I've derailed.
```

**What to expect:**
- The AI will ask when things went wrong
- It will dig into the root cause
- It will help you create a minimal recovery plan
- No judgment—just analysis

### Protocol E: Annual Review

**When to use:** End of year or start of new year

**How to start:**
```
Protocol E. Year in review.
```

**What to expect:**
- A long session (60-90 minutes)
- Review of who you actually became vs. planned
- Identification of patterns that persisted all year
- Setting direction for next year

### Protocol F: Mid-Quarter Pivot

**When to use:** Major life change, or you realize your goals are wrong

**How to start:**
```
Protocol F. Should I change course?
```

**What to expect:**
- The AI will check if this is a valid pivot or rationalized abandonment
- It will force you to articulate what changed
- You'll either adjust goals or confirm staying the course

### Protocol G: Success Analysis

**When to use:** You actually achieved a goal!

**How to start:**
```
Protocol G. I achieved [goal name].
```

**What to expect:**
- Honest analysis of why it worked
- Extraction of replicable patterns
- Comparison with goals that failed

## 7. Understanding the AI's Approach

### The 7 Principles the AI Uses

| Principle | What it means | How you'll experience it |
|-----------|---------------|--------------------------|
| **P1: Falsificationism** | Seeking evidence of failure | "What would prove this goal will fail?" |
| **P2: No Social Niceties** | Optimizing for truth | The AI won't sugarcoat things |
| **P3: Cognitive Bias Detection** | Naming your excuses | "That sounds like planning fallacy" |
| **P4: Binary Commitments** | Forcing trade-offs | "What are you saying NO to?" |
| **P5: Identity Focus** | Who, not what | "Who are you becoming?" |
| **P6: Pattern Matching** | Using your history | "You said this same thing last month" |
| **P7: Stoic Negative Visualization** | Anticipating failure | "What could go wrong?" |

### Why It Feels Uncomfortable

The AI is designed to challenge you. This can feel:
- Annoying ("Just accept my answer!")
- Frustrating ("Stop asking for specifics!")
- Revealing ("I didn't realize I do that...")

**This discomfort is the point.** Comfortable goal-setting leads to comfortable failure.

### When the AI Gets It Wrong

The AI isn't perfect. If it:
- Misunderstands something, correct it
- Pushes too hard, tell it to move on
- Gets stuck, say "Move to the next phase"

You're in control of the conversation.

**Weekly files:**
- Format: `YYYY-WNN.md`
- Example: `2026-W01.md` (Week 1 of 2026)

**Quarterly files:**
- Format: `YYYY-QN.md`

### What to Save

1. **The AI's [DONE] output** - This is your summary

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How often should I do each protocol?

| Protocol | Frequency |
|----------|-----------|
| A | Every quarter (4x per year) |
| B | Every week |
| C | Every month (or after 4-6 check-ins) |
| D | Whenever you've completely derailed |
| E | Once per year |
| F | When major circumstances change |
| G | When you achieve a goal |

### Q: What if I miss a weekly check-in?

Just do it when you can. If you miss multiple weeks, consider Protocol D (Crisis Recovery) to get back on track.

### Q: Can I use a different AI than ChatGPT?

Yes! Any AI that can follow instructions works:
- Claude (claude.ai)
- GPT-4
- Gemini
- Local LLMs

Just paste SYSTEM.md and trigger the protocol.

### Q: What if the AI doesn't follow the format?

Sometimes the AI doesn't use phase tags or doesn't end with [DONE]. You can:
1. Remind it: "Remember to use phase tags like [IDENTITY]"
2. Ask directly: "Now give me the [DONE] summary"
3. Move on: "Let's move to the next phase"

### Q: How do I handle sensitive goals?

The AI doesn't store your conversations permanently (on most platforms). But if you're concerned:
- Use a local LLM
- Be vague about specific details
- Focus on behaviors, not sensitive specifics

### Q: What if I want to change my goals mid-quarter?

Use Protocol F (Mid-Quarter Pivot). It will help you determine if this is a valid pivot or rationalized abandonment.

### Q: Do I need internet access?

You need internet to access ChatGPT or Claude. However:
- Your files are stored locally on your computer
- You can review your files offline anytime

## 10. Troubleshooting

### Problem: AI gives one-word answers or is too brief

**Solution:** Make sure you copied ALL of SYSTEM.md, including the Conversation Rules section.

### Problem: AI asks too many questions at once

**Solution:** Remind it:
```
Remember: ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next.
```

### Problem: AI is too nice and agreeable

**Solution:** Remind it:
```
You're supposed to be adversarial. Challenge me when my answers are vague.
```

### Problem: Conversation goes on forever

**Solution:** Say:
```
Let's move to the next phase now.
```
Or:
```
We've been on this topic long enough. What's next?
```

### Problem: AI doesn't use phase tags

**Solution:** Remind it:
```
Start each response with the phase tag in brackets, like [IDENTITY] or [GOALS].
```

### Problem: [DONE] output is incomplete

**Solution:** Ask directly:
```
Give me the complete [DONE] output with: ACTION ITEMS, PRINCIPLES APPLIED, and NEXT STEP.
```

### Problem: I don't know where my files are

**Solution:**
- On Windows: Check `Documents/personal-strategy/`
- On Mac: Check `Documents/personal-strategy/`
- Use your computer's search function to find "SYSTEM.md"
